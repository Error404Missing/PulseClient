04 — Questions

Answers, before you ask.

How do I get my license key? +

Open a ticket on our Discord server or message the Telegram bot and ask for a license. The team issues it free of charge — no purchase, no card, no waiting list.

Where do I enter the key? +

When you launch Minecraft with the mod installed, an activation window appears. Paste your key there and press Activate. The key is stored locally and reused on every launch.

Can I use one key on more than one PC? +

Each key is bound to the HWID of the first device that activates it. To move it to a new PC, ask support in Discord for a free HWID reset.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I get my license key? +

Where should I enter the key to play? +

When you start Minecraft, a special dark window will appear asking you for a key. Type it there and click Activate! The key will be automatically added and saved.

To prevent key sharing, each key is associated with the HWID of the first computer it is activated on. You can resolve the HWID by contacting the support group on our Discord server.
