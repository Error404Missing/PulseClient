01Cookies vs. local storage

A cookie is a small file a site asks your browser to store and send back with later requests. Local storage and session storage work similarly but stay inside your browser and are not sent with every request. This policy covers all three, because the rules on consent apply to all of them.

02Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary — required to sign you in, keep your session secure, protect the site from bots and remember your cookie choice. These cannot be switched off without breaking the site.
  • Preferences / functional — remember your last dashboard tab, language, dismissed notices and referral code so the interface does not reset itself.
  • Analytics — none are set today. If we add a privacy-friendly analytics tool later, it will stay off until you opt in.
  • Marketing — none. We do not run advertising pixels, retargeting or social trackers.

04Third-party requests that receive your IP

Even without cookies, loading a resource from another server reveals your IP address and user agent to that server. The public site makes these requests:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — the typefaces used across the site.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — serves the Supabase browser SDK.
  • Discord CDN (cdn.discordapp.com) — profile avatars shown in the dashboard.
  • mc-heads.net — Minecraft head and skin renders.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, caching and security for the whole site.

Signed-in users also reach our API (api.pulseclient.xyz, with a Hugging Face Spaces fallback) and, during security checks, IP intelligence services (freeipapi.com, ipify.org, IPinfo blackbox, ipquery.io, ipwho.is) that receive the IP being checked. The client download is streamed from GitHub. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

05Analytics, advertising and Do Not Track

We do not load Google Analytics, ad pixels, session recorders, heatmaps or social plugins. Because there is nothing to opt out of, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change what the site loads. Optional analytics and marketing categories in our consent banner are off by default and currently have no scripts attached.

06How to change or withdraw your choice

  • Use the Cookie settings button in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and choose again.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove every item listed above; you will be signed out.
  • Block third-party cookies or fonts in your browser settings — the site still works, but icons and type may fall back to system defaults.
  • Blocking strictly necessary storage means you cannot stay signed in, and license features will not load.

Withdrawing consent never affects processing that already happened, and it does not remove records we must keep for security or accounting reasons (see the Privacy Policy).
