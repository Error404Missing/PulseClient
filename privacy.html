Privacy Policy

What PulseClient collects when you use the website, sign in with Discord or run the client — and what you can do about it.

Last updated: 17 November 2026  ·  Version 1.0

01Who we are

PulseClient ("PulseClient", "we", "us") operates pulseclient.xyz and the PulseClient desktop client. We decide how and why the personal data described below is processed, which makes us the data controller for that data.

You can reach us for any privacy question or request at pulseclient.official@gmail.com. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.

02Data we collect

Account data (Discord sign-in)

Signing in uses Discord OAuth through our authentication provider, Supabase. We receive and store your Discord user ID, username and display name, avatar reference, and the email address Discord shares with the OAuth application. We also check which roles you hold in our Discord server to decide whether you get staff or owner access. We never receive your Discord password.

License and device data

When a license is created, linked or activated we store the license key, its status and expiry, the Discord account it is linked to, the hardware identifier (HWID) reported by the client, your device slot usage, HWID reset history and any internal notes added by staff. HWID values are truncated in the dashboard but stored in full to enforce one-device licensing.

Security and anti-abuse data

To keep the service fair and available we process your IP address, approximate location derived from it, ISP/ASN, browser user agent, request timestamps, and signals such as "VPN/proxy/Tor detected" returned by IP intelligence services. We keep records of bans, blacklist entries, alternate-account checks and admin actions. This data is visible to staff in our internal panel and in the audit matrix.

Diagnostics from the desktop client

The client can send crash reports, client logs, a summary of your Minecraft installation, and — when a staff member explicitly requests a diagnostic session — a screenshot and system information. Staff can also send commands to a running client for support and moderation purposes. Diagnostics are used to fix bugs, investigate abuse and answer support tickets.

Support and community content

Messages you send through Discord tickets, the Telegram bot or staff direct messages, including attachments, are stored so we can resolve your request. Content you submit to community sections (for example configs, media links or bot skins) is stored and may be shown publicly.

Invoices and contact details

If staff issues an invoice for a license, we process the buyer's email address and Discord ID, and deliver the invoice through our transactional email provider. We do not run a payment card form on this website.

Technical logs

Our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) processes request metadata — IP address, requested URL, timestamp, user agent, and security signals — to serve and protect the site. We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site analytics trackers.

03Why we use it (legal bases)

  • Providing the service — creating your account, linking and validating licenses, enforcing device limits and keeping you signed in. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Safety and fair use — detecting alternate accounts, VPN/proxy abuse, ban evasion and license sharing; rate limiting and fraud prevention. Legal basis: our legitimate interests in protecting the service and its users.
  • Support and diagnostics — crash reports, logs and requested diagnostic sessions. Legal basis: contract, and consent where a session is initiated at your request.
  • Invoices and accounting — issuing and retaining invoice records. Legal basis: contract and legal obligation.
  • Service messages — security alerts, license changes and important product notices. Legal basis: contract or legitimate interests.
  • Optional features — anything not covered above (for example optional analytics, if we ever add them) runs only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for cross-site advertising.

04Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage that is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences, plus a small record of your cookie choice. No advertising or analytics cookies are set by the site today. When you first visit, a banner asks you to accept all optional categories or keep only what is necessary; you can change that choice any time from the footer.

Every item we store, its purpose and its lifetime is listed in the Cookie Policy.

06International transfers

Our providers are mainly established in the United States and the European Union. Where personal data leaves the EEA or the UK, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, together with encryption in transit.

07How long we keep it

  • Account and profile — while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of a valid deletion request (backups rotate within 90 days).
  • License, activation and HWID history — up to 24 months after the license expires, to resolve disputes and prevent license abuse.
  • Security logs, bans and blacklist records — up to 24 months; ban records may be kept longer where needed to prevent ban evasion.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic sessions — up to 90 days unless attached to an open support case.
  • Support tickets — up to 24 months after the ticket closes.
  • Invoices — as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Consent record — until you clear it; we may ask again after 12 months.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your account and the data attached to it;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting processing that already happened;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Email pulseclient.official@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or open a Discord ticket. We may ask you to verify that you control the Discord account before we act. Some records — for example an active ban or an invoice we are legally required to keep — cannot be erased immediately; we will tell you which and why.

09Security

Traffic is encrypted with TLS, administrative access is limited to named staff accounts with role checks, and access to logs and tickets is audited. We keep the data we need and delete the rest on the schedule above. No system is perfectly secure: if you find a vulnerability, please report it to pulseclient.official@gmail.com (our published security.txt contact) instead of testing it against other users.

10Automated checks and appeals

Some connections are screened automatically: VPN, proxy or Tor exit nodes and blacklisted IP ranges may be blocked before sign-in completes, and alternate-account detection may flag a license for review. These checks can affect your access without a human reading every case first. You can appeal any automated block by opening a ticket in our Discord server or messaging the Telegram bot; a staff member will review it.

We do not use automated processing to make decisions that produce legal effects beyond access to PulseClient itself.

11Children

PulseClient is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age for consenting to online services, that age applies.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The version and date at the top always reflect the current text, and material changes will be announced in our Discord server. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
